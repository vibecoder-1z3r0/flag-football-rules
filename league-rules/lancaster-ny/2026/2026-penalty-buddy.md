# Lancaster NY Flag Football — 2026 Penalty Buddy (Quick Reference)

*Print at 50% scale.*

## Offensive Penalties

| Enforcement | Fouls |
|---|---|
| -5 yards from LOS, Replay the Down | Offsides / False Start · Illegal Forward Pass / Illegal Rush · Pass Interference · Illegal Motion · Impeding the Rusher · Illegal Procedure · Taunting |
| -5 yards from LOS, Loss of Down | Delay of Game (Clock Stops) |
| -5 yards from Spot of Foul, Loss of Down | Flag Guarding |
| -10 yards from LOS, Loss of Down | Unsportsmanlike Conduct |
| -10 yards from Spot of Foul, Loss of Down | Screening, Blocking · Charging · Unnecessary Roughness |

## Defensive Penalties

| Enforcement | Fouls |
|---|---|
| +5 yards from LOS, Replay the Down | Offsides · Illegal Rush · Delay of Game (Clock Stops) |
| +5 yards from LOS or End of Play, Automatic 1st Down | Illegal Flag Pull · Roughing the Passer · Holding · Taunting |
| Spot of Foul, Automatic 1st Down | Pass Interference |
| +10 yards from LOS or End of Play, Automatic 1st Down | Stripping · Unsportsmanlike Conduct · Unnecessary Roughness |
