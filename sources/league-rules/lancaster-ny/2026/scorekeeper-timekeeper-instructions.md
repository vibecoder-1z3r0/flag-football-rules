# Score Sheet Instructions (Score/Timekeeper) — 2026

Instructions printed on the score sheet for the scorekeeper/timekeeper.

The games are 25 minute halves, running clock, each team gets two time-outs per half.

Talk with your referee to determine who will manage the "actual game clock." Announce the
clock time to the referee with regular frequency.

If a team calls a time-out, check a time-out off the score card and remind the referee how many
timeouts that team has left. If the team called a time-out and they had zero time-outs, notify the
referee immediately.

After each touchdown / conversion attempt, announce the score to the referee and to each
team's coaches.

The finalized score sheet should be given to the coach of the winning team.

Fill out the team name under each team block in the first half section.

Fill out the Score Keeper's name with your name and the field number.
