# Lancaster NY Flag Football — 2026 General Rules & Coach Guidelines

> Divisions: Peewee (PW), Freshman, Girls Freshman, JV, Varsity, Girls JV.
> Modifications layered on top of the USA Football base rules (`base-rules/`).

## Uniforms / Equipment

- Co-ed: Home = Red, Away = Black
- Girls: Home = Red, Away = White
- Post which jersey your team will wear for their game on SportsEngine
- All players are required to wear mouthguards

## Game

- 5 vs 5
- Two 25-minute halves
- 5-minute halftime
- Each team gets 2 timeouts per half (unused timeouts do not carry over)
- Visiting team calls the coin toss — NO DEFERRALS

## Clock and Timing Rules

- The game clock is a continuous clock
- The referee controls the clock
- The clock stops at timeouts
- The referee can stop the clock at their discretion
- If the clock has been stopped, it starts at the snap of the next legal down (an extra point is
  not a legal down — if a coach calls a timeout after a touchdown, the extra point is an untimed
  down)
- A 30-second play clock starts when the official spots the ball, or after a return-to-play whistle
- If a coach calls an illegal timeout, there is a 10-second clock runoff if the loss of time is
  disadvantageous to the team calling the timeout (i.e., the team calling the timeout would win
  if the game ended when the runoff ended), in addition to a delay-of-game penalty.

## Coaches on the Field

- Peewee — 2 coaches allowed on field and in the huddle
- Freshman — 1 coach allowed on field and in the huddle
- JV and Varsity — no coaches allowed on the field or in the huddle
- Girls Freshman and JV — 1 coach allowed on field and in the huddle

## Offense

### Down and Distance

- 4 downs to cross midfield — may punt on 4th down (ball moves to opponent's 5-yard line)
- 4 downs to score after crossing midfield; failure to score is a turnover on downs, opposing
  team starts possession from that spot
- The offense may punt the ball on any down (ball moves to the opponent's 5-yard line)

### Failure to Convert

If the offense goes for it on 4th down and fails to achieve the line to gain or cross the goal line,
the opposing team starts possession from that spot — unless the ball is within the no-run zone
near the defending team's end zone, in which case the ball is placed at the 5-yard line.

### Point After Touchdown

1 point (5-yard line, PASS only); 2 points (10-yard line, RUN or PASS).

### Snapping the Ball

All age groups must snap the ball between their legs, except Peewee and Girls Freshman, who
may side-snap as needed.

### Botched Snap / Exchange

- **Peewee, Freshman, Girls Freshman**: any botched/fumbled exchange is a do-over, up to two
  times per half, with no loss of down or yardage. After that, loss of down, no loss of yardage.
- **JV, Varsity**: any botched/fumbled exchange results in loss of down but no loss of yardage.
  If a botched snap occurs in the end zone, it results in a safety (opposing team gets 2 points
  and the ball).

### Passing / QB / Motion Rules

- All passes must cross the line of scrimmage. No pitches or passes behind the line of
  scrimmage. Any pass that does not cross the LOS, whether received or not, is illegal (5-yard
  penalty).
- Forward progress is determined by the ball carrier's frontmost part of their frontmost foot
  when their flag is pulled (or, in certain cases, the furthest-upfield body part that caused the
  player to be down). The ball NEVER determines forward progress.
- No laterals or pitch-outs — handoffs only.
- The quarterback has 7 seconds to get rid of the ball; the ref keeps an internal clock and blows
  the play dead if the QB hasn't released the ball in time.
- If the QB hands the ball off, that player is eligible to throw but must be behind the LOS and
  the pass must cross the LOS. If there is no blitz by the defense, the ball carrier must decide
  to run or throw within the 7-second clock.
- The QB can send multiple players in motion, but only one may be in motion at the snap; all
  others must be set.
- QBs cannot run the ball.

### Turnovers

- There are no fumbles — once the ball hits the ground, the play is dead.
- Any interception during regular play can be returned for 6 points.
- Any interception on a conversion can be returned for 2 points.

### Successful Reception

- A player who gains possession of the ball in the air is considered inbounds as long as the
  first foot comes down inbounds.
- Also true if a player makes a catch with at least one foot inbounds before going out of
  bounds.

### Dead Ball

A play is ruled dead when the ball hits the ground, the ball carrier's knee or arm contacts the
field, or the ball carrier leaves their feet.

## No Run Zone

The area 5 yards prior to midfield and prior to the end zone. The ball must be thrown in this
zone — no run plays. No Run Zones prevent teams from conducting power run plays. A penalty
is enforced for running in the no-run zone.

## Defense

### Lining Up

- **PW, Freshman, Girls**: all non-blitzing players must be at least 5 yards back from the LOS,
  and may proceed to the LOS at the snap but not cross it. They can meet the ball carrier at the
  LOS to track down QBs or ball carriers. Only the blitzer is allowed behind the LOS (see rusher
  rules below).
- **JV and Varsity**: defensive players can line up anywhere.

### Pass Rushing

The rusher must have BOTH FEET behind the puck. If the rusher leaves the rush line before the
snap and crosses the LOS, this is a 5-yard penalty and replay of down (Illegal Rush). The rusher
must cross the LOS in pursuit of the QB for this penalty to be called. There is no illegal rush if
the ball has been handed off.

- **Freshmen and Peewee**: rusher must be 10 yards off the LOS (ref drops a puck to mark 10
  yards each play). May rush as many defenders as desired, as long as behind the 10-yard rush
  puck.
- **JV and Varsity**: rusher must be 7 yards off the LOS (ref drops a puck to mark 7 yards each
  play). May rush as many defenders as desired, as long as behind the 7-yard rush puck.

## Overtime

Any game tied at the end of regulation goes to overtime — the league wants to avoid ties for
standings.

Each team gets one chance from the 5-yard line to pass for a 1-point conversion. If one team
succeeds and the other does not, the team that scored wins. If neither succeeds, or both are
tied after 1 round, each team gets the ball from the 10-yard line and the process continues until
a winner emerges.

## Penalties

### Defense

| Foul | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Offside | +5 yards, replay of down |
| Illegal Rush | +5 yards, replay of down |
| Delay of Game | +5 yards, replay of down, clock stoppage |
| Holding | +5 yards from the spot, automatic first down |
| Pass Interference | Spot foul, automatic first down |
| Roughing the Passer | +5 yards, automatic first down |
| Unsportsmanlike Conduct (personal foul) | +10 yards, automatic first down |
| Unnecessary Roughness (personal foul) | +10 yards, automatic first down |

### Offense

| Foul | Penalty |
|---|---|
| False Start | -5 yards, replay of down |
| Illegal Forward Pass | -5 yards, replay of down |
| Illegal Motion | -5 yards, replay of down |
| Impeding the Rusher | -5 yards, replay of down |
| Screening / Blocking | Spot foul, -10 yards from spot, loss of down |
| Flag Guarding | -5 yards from the spot, loss of down |
| Unsportsmanlike Conduct (personal foul) | -10 yards, loss of down |
| Unnecessary Roughness (personal foul) | -10 yards, loss of down |
| Delay of Game | -5 yards, loss of down, clock stoppage |

> Once a play is past the line of scrimmage via a pass or run, all other offensive players without
> the ball must come to a complete stop.

**Unnecessary roughness** is a safety-related personal foul where a player uses excessive force
and/or makes unnecessary contact — including but not limited to tackling/pushing/dragging a
player to the ground, lowering the shoulder, intentionally running into a player while making no
attempt to pull the flag, tripping, or pushing a player out of bounds.

**Personal fouls**: a player's first personal foul is a warning. A second personal foul by the same
player results in ejection from the game.

### Edge Case — Flag Guarding After Gaining a First Down

If a runner has "gained the first down" but then flag-guards afterward on the same play (e.g., 5
yards past the first-down line), a flag-guarding penalty at the spot is assessed as follows: a first
down is awarded for the drive (that team cannot gain another first down on the current drive —
the no-run zone is 5 yards from the end zone), and the penalty yardage is assessed. The next
legal play is "first down and goal".
