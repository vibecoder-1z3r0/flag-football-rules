# Lancaster NY Flag Football — 2026 Coaches Cheat Sheet

> Condensed version of `2026-general-rules-and-guidelines.md` for sideline reference. Content
> matches the general rules doc; this file only calls out details that are stated here but not
> spelled out there.

## Uniforms / Equipment

- Co-ed: Home = Red, Away = Black
- Girls: Home = Red, Away = White
- Post which jersey your team will wear on SportsEngine
- All players required to wear mouthguards

## Game

- 5 vs 5, two 25-minute halves (continuous clock)
- 30-second play clock on offense, 2 timeouts per half
- Referee controls the clock

## Down and Distance

- 4 downs to cross midfield — may punt on 4th down (ball to opponent's 5-yard line)
- 4 downs to score after crossing midfield; failure to score is a turnover on downs at that spot

## Failure to Convert

If the offense goes for it on 4th down and doesn't cross midfield or fails to score, the opposing
team starts possession from that spot.

## Point After Touchdown

1 point (5-yard line, PASS only); 2 points (10-yard line, RUN or PASS).

## Defense

- **PW, Freshman, Girls**: non-blitzing players must be at least 5 yards back from the LOS, may
  proceed to the LOS at the snap but not cross it. Only the blitzer is allowed behind the LOS.
- **JV and Varsity**: defensive players can line up anywhere.
- **Tackle spot**: the referee spots the ball according to where the ball carrier's feet are when
  the flag is pulled (not where the ball is).

### Freshmen and Peewee Pass Rushers

The rusher must raise a hand and identify themself as the blitzer, and must be 10 yards off the
LOS (ref drops a puck to mark it each play). Both feet must stay behind the puck. Leaving the
rush line before the snap and crossing the LOS is a 5-yard penalty and replay of down (Illegal
Rush).

### JV and Varsity Pass Rushers

Must be 7 yards off the LOS (ref drops a puck to mark it each play). Any number of defenders
may rush as long as they stay behind the 7-yard rush puck.

## Coaches on the Field

- Peewee — 2 coaches allowed on field and in the huddle
- Freshman — 1 coach allowed on field and in the huddle
- JV and Varsity — no coaches allowed on the field or in the huddle
- Girls Freshman and JV — 1 coach allowed on field and in the huddle

## Snapping the Ball

All age groups snap between the legs, except Peewee and Girls Freshman, who may side-snap
as needed.

## Botched Snap / Exchange

- **Peewee, Freshman, Girls Freshman**: do-over up to two times per half, no loss of down or
  yardage. After that, loss of down (no yardage loss).
- **JV, Varsity**: loss of down, no yardage loss. If in the end zone: safety (opponent gets 2 points
  and the ball).

## Passing / QB / Motion Rules

- All passes must cross the LOS. No pitches or passes behind the LOS — any pass that doesn't
  cross the LOS (received or not) is illegal, 5-yard penalty.
- No laterals or pitch-outs — handoffs only.
- If there is no blitz, the QB has 7 seconds to get rid of the ball; ref keeps an internal clock and
  blows the play dead if time expires.
- If the QB hands off, that player may throw but must be behind the LOS with the pass crossing
  the LOS; if there's no blitz, the ball carrier must decide to run or throw within the 7-second
  clock.
- QB can send multiple players in motion; only one may be in motion at the snap, others set.
- QBs cannot run the ball.

## Turnovers

- No fumbles — ball dead the instant it hits the ground.
- Interceptions during regular play returnable for 6 points; interceptions on a conversion
  returnable for 2 points.

## Live / Dead Ball

- A player who gains possession in the air is inbounds as long as the first foot down is inbounds.
- Play is dead when the ball hits the ground, the ball carrier's knee or arm contacts the field, or
  the ball carrier leaves their feet.

## No Run Zone

5 yards prior to midfield and prior to the end zone — ball must be thrown; penalty for running in
the zone.

## Overtime

Game tied at the end of regulation goes to overtime (league wants to avoid ties for standings).
Each team gets one chance from the 5-yard line to pass for a 1-point conversion. If one team
succeeds and the other doesn't, the scoring team wins. If neither succeeds, or both tie after a
round, both teams get the ball from the 10-yard line and the process repeats until a winner
emerges.

## Penalties

### Defense

- Offside: +5 yards, replay of down
- Illegal Rush: +5 yards, replay of down
- Holding: +5 yards from spot, automatic first down
- Pass Interference: spot foul, automatic first down
- Roughing the Passer: +5 yards, automatic first down
- Unsportsmanlike Conduct (personal foul): +10 yards, automatic first down
- Unnecessary Roughness (personal foul): +10 yards, automatic first down

### Offense

- False Start: -5 yards, replay of down
- Illegal Forward Pass: -5 yards, replay of down
- Illegal Motion: -5 yards, replay of down
- Impeding the Rusher: -5 yards, replay of down
- Screening / Blocking: spot foul, -10 yards from spot, loss of down (once a play is past the LOS
  via pass or run, all other offensive players without the ball must come to a complete stop)
- Flag Guarding: -5 yards from spot, loss of down
- Unsportsmanlike Conduct (personal foul): -10 yards, loss of down
- Unnecessary Roughness (personal foul): -10 yards, loss of down
- **Taking more than 30 seconds to snap the ball between plays**: -5 yards, loss of down

**Unnecessary roughness** = a safety-related personal foul involving excessive force/unnecessary
contact (tackling/pushing/dragging a player down, lowering the shoulder, intentionally running
into a player with no attempt to pull the flag, tripping, pushing a player out of bounds).

**Personal fouls**: first is a warning; a second personal foul by the same player results in
ejection.
