# Penalty Matrix

Single canonical penalty table. This is the only place penalty yardage/enforcement is stated —
`rulebook.md` links here rather than repeating numbers per-rule. Source: reconciled from the
2026 General Rules & Guidelines, 2026 Coaches Cheat Sheet, and 2026 Penalty Buddy card
(consistent with each other except where flagged), overriding the USA Football baseline where
they differ.

**Enforcement terms:**
- **Replay of down** — down is not consumed; offense repeats the same down.
- **Loss of down** — down is consumed; offense moves to the next down (or turnover on downs
  if it was 4th down).
- **Spot foul** — enforced from the spot of the foul, not the line of scrimmage.
- **Automatic first down** — offense (defense's foul) is awarded a new set of downs regardless
  of yardage gained.

## General Enforcement Rules

- All penalties are assessed from the line of scrimmage except spot fouls (marked above).
- Live-ball penalties are enforced before the down is considered complete.
- If penalty yardage would move the ball more than half the distance to the goal, assess only
  half the distance to the goal.
- Penalty yardage may not push the ball past the 40-yard line into the Administrative Zone.
- A penalty occurring at the 40-yard line results in loss of down (or replay, per the foul) with no
  yardage lost.
- Games may not end on a defensive penalty unless the offense declines it.
- Personal fouls (Unsportsmanlike Conduct, Unnecessary Roughness) carry a progressive-
  discipline consequence independent of yardage: a player's first personal foul is a warning; a
  second by the same player is an ejection. See `rulebook.md` §12.2.

See `officials-manual.md` §OM-7 for administrative mechanics (who calls what, live-ball vs.
dead-ball sequencing, etc.).
