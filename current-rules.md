# Current Rules — Lancaster NY Flag Football (2026 Season)

This is the merged, authoritative rule set for the current season: the USA Football base
rulebook, overridden/extended by the Lancaster NY 2026 league rules, plus pending custom
additions. **Where a Lancaster rule conflicts with the base rulebook, the Lancaster rule wins.**

Sources (in precedence order, highest wins):
1. `league-rules/lancaster-ny/2026/custom-additions.md` — pending house rules, not yet final
2. `league-rules/lancaster-ny/2026/2026-general-rules-and-guidelines.md` — current season league rules
3. `base-rules/usa-football-flag-rulebook.md` — national baseline

`league-rules/lancaster-ny/2024/` is prior-season history only; no longer authoritative.

---

## Game Format

- 5 vs 5, two 25-minute halves, continuous/running clock, referee-controlled
- 5-minute halftime
- 2 timeouts per team per half (30 seconds each, do not carry over between halves)
- Visiting team calls the coin toss — **no deferrals**
- 30-second play clock, starts when the ball is spotted or after a "return to play" whistle

## Divisions

Peewee (PW), Freshman, Girls Freshman, JV, Varsity, Girls JV. Several rules below vary by
division — noted inline.

## Uniforms / Equipment

- Co-ed: Home = Red, Away = Black. Girls: Home = Red, Away = White.
- Post jersey color for the game on SportsEngine.
- Mouthguards required for all players.
- (Base rulebook equipment rules — no metal cleats, no jewelry, flags not same color as
  shorts/pants, etc. — still apply; see base rulebook §3.)

## Downs

- **4 downs** to cross midfield (base rulebook uses 3; Lancaster overrides to 4).
- **4 downs** to score after crossing midfield.
- Offense may punt on any down; a 4th-down punt moves the ball to the opponent's 5-yard line.
- **Peewee**: no go/punt decision — 4 downs to the line-to-gain, then another 4 downs to the
  goal line automatically.
- **FR and JV**: on 4th down, the coach must decide *before the snap* whether to go for it or
  punt.
  - **Pending addition** (not yet finalized — see `custom-additions.md`): the coach gets ~5
    seconds to declare that decision. Timing relationship to the 30-second play clock, the
    default if no decision is made in time, and whether this extends to all divisions are still
    open questions.
- Failure to convert (fails to reach the line-to-gain or score): opposing team takes over at that
  spot, **except** within the no-run zone near the defending team's end zone, where the ball is
  placed at the 5-yard line instead.

## Scoring

- Touchdown: 6 points
- PAT: 1 point (5-yard line, **pass only**) or 2 points (10-yard line, run or pass)
- Interceptions: returnable for 6 points during regular play; returnable for 2 points on a PAT/
  conversion attempt (base rulebook says conversion picks are dead immediately with no
  return — **Lancaster 2026 overrides this**: conversion picks ARE returnable, for 2 points)
- No fumbles — ball is dead the instant it touches the ground (no live recovery)
- Botched snap/exchange:
  - **Peewee, Freshman, Girls Freshman**: do-over, up to 2 times per half, no loss of down/
    yardage; after that, loss of down only
  - **JV, Varsity**: loss of down, no yardage loss; if in the end zone, it's a safety (opponent
    gets 2 points and the ball)

## Overtime — 2026 Format (replaces base rulebook overtime entirely)

Tied at the end of regulation → each team gets one attempt from the 5-yard line to pass for a
1-point conversion.
- One succeeds, other doesn't → scoring team wins immediately.
- Both succeed or both fail → both teams move to the 10-yard line and repeat until a winner
  emerges.

*(This differs substantially from the base rulebook's alternating-choice, escalating 1-vs-2-point
overtime format — the 2026 Lancaster format is simpler: both teams always attempt from the
same distance each round, no offense/defense coin-toss choice.)*

## Running

- Handoffs only — **no laterals, no pitches, no screen passes of any kind.**
- QB cannot run the ball.
- Forward progress = ball carrier's frontmost foot at the moment the flag is pulled (or the
  furthest-upfield body part that grounds them). The ball itself never determines forward
  progress.
- No-run zone: 5 yards before midfield (for the first down) and 5 yards before the end zone (for
  the score) — must pass in these zones.

## Passing

- All passes must cross the line of scrimmage; anything that doesn't (received or not) is an
  illegal forward pass, 5-yard penalty and replay of down.
- QB has a 7-second pass clock. Officials keep an internal count; if the QB hasn't released the
  ball by 7 seconds, the play is dead.
- If the ball is handed off, that player may throw — must be behind the LOS, pass must cross
  the LOS. If there's no blitz, the ball carrier must still decide run-or-throw within the 7-second
  window.
- Motion: multiple players may go in motion pre-snap, but only one may still be moving at the
  snap; all others must be set.

## Defense / Rushing the Passer

- **PW, Freshman, Girls**: non-blitzing defenders must start 5 yards back from the LOS and
  can't cross it until the snap. Only the designated blitzer may line up behind the LOS.
  Freshmen/Peewee blitzers must raise a hand to identify themselves before the play and must
  keep both feet behind a puck placed 10 yards off the LOS.
- **JV, Varsity**: defenders may line up anywhere. Rushers must keep both feet behind a puck
  placed 7 yards off the LOS. Any number of defenders may rush.
- Leaving the rush line early and crossing the LOS before a handoff/pass = illegal rush, 5 yards
  and replay of down. No illegal rush once the ball has been handed off.
- Ball is spotted where the ball carrier's feet were when the flag was pulled — not where the
  ball is.

## Flag Pulling / Flag Guarding

Same as base rulebook (§14): legal pull requires full possession, no stripping, no diving through
the runner. Flag guarding = stiff-arming, dropping the head/arm/shoulder, or covering flags with
the jersey to block access — penalized as a spot foul.

**Edge case**: if a runner gains the first down, then flag-guards later on the same play, the first
down still counts (drive continues at "first down and goal" if applicable) but the flag-guarding
penalty yardage is still assessed from the spot.

## Penalties

### Defense

| Foul | Enforcement |
|---|---|
| Offside | +5 yards, replay of down |
| Illegal Rush | +5 yards, replay of down |
| Delay of Game | +5 yards, replay of down, clock stoppage |
| Holding | +5 yards from spot, automatic first down |
| Pass Interference | Spot foul, automatic first down |
| Roughing the Passer | +5 yards, automatic first down |
| Unsportsmanlike Conduct (personal foul) | +10 yards, automatic first down |
| Unnecessary Roughness (personal foul) | +10 yards, automatic first down |

### Offense

| Foul | Enforcement |
|---|---|
| False Start / Offside | -5 yards, replay of down |
| Illegal Forward Pass | -5 yards, replay of down |
| Illegal Motion | -5 yards, replay of down |
| Impeding the Rusher | -5 yards, replay of down |
| Delay of game (taking >30s to snap between plays) | -5 yards, loss of down |
| Screening / Blocking | Spot foul, -10 yards from spot, loss of down |
| Flag Guarding | -5 yards from spot, loss of down |
| Unsportsmanlike Conduct (personal foul) | -10 yards, loss of down |
| Unnecessary Roughness (personal foul) | -10 yards, loss of down |

- Once a play crosses the LOS via pass or run, all offensive players without the ball must come
  to a complete stop (no live blocking/screening downfield).
- **Unnecessary roughness** = safety-related personal foul: tackling/pushing/dragging a player
  down, lowering the shoulder, intentionally running into a player with no attempt to pull the
  flag, tripping, or pushing a player out of bounds.
- **Personal fouls**: first offense is a warning; a second personal foul by the same player =
  ejection.

## Officiating

- 2-puck system: one marks the line of scrimmage, one marks the rush line.
- Only the team captain or head coach may question a ruling — a coach may burn a timeout to
  ask for a rules clarification; if the ruling favors the coach (or was a legitimate question), the
  timeout can be refunded.
- Rules debates happen at midfield with the ref, both head coaches, and Lancaster Flag
  Administration if available.
- Officials should teach/warn early in the season ("next time it will be enforced") and move to
  straight enforcement as the season progresses.
